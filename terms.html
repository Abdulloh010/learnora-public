Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account data: First name, last name, username, email address, grade/year, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never see your plain-text password).
  • Profile data: Bio, location, skills, experiences, and profile cover preferences.
  • Content: Posts, comments, quiz answers, and messages you send.

Information collected automatically:

  • XP points, quiz scores, and activity data generated from your use of the Platform.
  • Last active timestamp to support features like streaks.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To personalise your learning experience and show relevant content
  • To send transactional emails (email verification, password reset)
  • To display your profile, posts, and activity to other users
  • To calculate XP, levels, and leaderboard rankings
  • To enable connections and messaging between users
  • To improve Platform features and user experience

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases:

  • Other users: Your profile name, username, grade, bio, location, posts, and quiz history are visible to other registered users of the Platform.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Email delivery: We use SMTP services to deliver transactional emails. These services only receive your email address and message content.

Your password, messages, and email address are kept private and are not shown to other users.

4. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot recover your plain-text password.
  • Authentication uses secure session tokens with expiry dates.
  • API communication uses Content-Type validation and authorisation headers.
  • SQL queries use prepared statements to prevent injection attacks.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your own credentials.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & portability: You may request a copy of your personal data by contacting us.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information at any time from Settings.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Danger Zone. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Password change: You can change your password at any time from Settings.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your session token, last visited page, and onboarding preferences locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to third parties.

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are used on the Platform.

7. Children's Privacy

Learnora is an educational platform intended for students of all ages, including those under 13. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information without consent may contact us at support@learnora.uz to request removal.
