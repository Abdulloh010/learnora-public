How to get started

1

Create Your Account

Sign up as a student first. Set your grade level and explore the platform to understand what students need.

2

Apply in the Learn Page

Once logged in, go to Learn and click "Become a Teacher". Fill out your application — subject expertise, experience, and a short bio.

3

Complete Teacher Training

Read our teaching guidelines and best practices. A quick training module ensures quality sessions for all students.

4

Start Teaching!

Access your Teacher Hub to schedule sessions, create courses, and manage student registrations — all in one place.

Teacher Hub Features
  • Schedule & manage live sessions
  • Create structured multi-session courses
  • Track student attendance & progress
  • View ratings and student feedback
  • Set max students & intro questions
